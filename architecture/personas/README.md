# Personas

## Primary Internal Users

-   Enrollment Specialist --- convert enrollment information into an
    accurate Patient Support Plan.
-   Care Coordinator --- manage ongoing patient support.
-   Care Coordinator Manager --- workload, escalations, quality and
    visibility.
-   Field Reimbursement Manager (FRM) --- collaborate with
    HCP/HCO/Payer/Site of Care on access/reimbursement work.

## External Actors

Patient, HCP, HCO/Site of Care, Payer and future ecosystem participants.

External actors are not automatically authenticated Salesforce users in
the MVP.
